# ShopSphere — Premium Motion Design Direction

ShopSphere will be presented as a premium performance-joggers brand, not a basic internship template.

## Reference translation
The supplied references use cinematic logo reveals, gradient scene transitions, floating interface cards, layered depth, smooth UI assembly, status verification, bold minimal typography, and a polished final brand reveal.

ShopSphere will use the same motion principles in an original joggers-focused experience without copying third-party branding or UI.

## Homepage scenes
1. Brand loader and logo reveal
2. Full-screen hero jogger with kinetic typography
3. Scroll-following product story
4. Product anatomy: upper, cushioning, midsole, outsole
5. Running, lifestyle, training, and everyday collections
6. Django-driven featured products
7. Trust, delivery, reviews, and verification cards
8. Final campaign statement and animated footer

## Visual language
- Black and warm off-white base
- Electric blue accent
- Multi-color gradient environments
- Large editorial typography
- Rounded glass cards
- Controlled glow, shadow, grain, and negative space

## Motion rules
- Motion explains content and never blocks shopping
- Reduced-motion accessibility is supported
- Mobile receives simplified premium animation
- Product content remains connected to Django
- Cart, checkout, accounts, and orders stay fully functional
