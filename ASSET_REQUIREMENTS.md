# ShopSphere Asset Requirements

## Prepared in this pack
- Original ShopSphere vector mark and wordmark
- Transparent hero jogger vector
- Alternate product-angle vectors
- Blue, white, and neon colorways
- Sole and upper close-up vectors
- Original ambient MP4 motion loops
- Grain, grid, and gradient-mesh textures
- Premium homepage CSS and JavaScript foundations
- Reference-video contact sheet for private motion study
- PowerShell installer and Git commit helper

## Final photography replacement specification
Replace temporary vector shoes with one consistent real product photographed in matching angles:

1. Hero three-quarter angle
2. Side profile
3. Top view
4. Rear view
5. Outsole close-up
6. Upper-material close-up
7. Cushioning close-up
8. Matching colorways

### Images
- PNG or WebP
- Transparent or clean studio background
- Minimum 1600 px width
- Consistent camera, lighting, and product
- No third-party logos or watermarks
- Rights cleared for portfolio and deployment

### Videos
- MP4, optionally WebM
- 1920×1080 preferred
- 5–12 second loops
- Running, lacing, impact, or lifestyle movement
- No logos, subtitles, timestamps, or watermarks
- Rights cleared for public deployment

## Important
The `reference/` directory is private study material and must not be deployed publicly.
